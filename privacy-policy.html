Privacy Policy

How we handle information and the terms on which we work.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Gwadar Digital Agency (we, us) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.gwadar.digital, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and message when you fill in a form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp. We may also collect basic technical data such as your browser, device and pages visited, through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide a quote or service.
  • To improve our website, services and marketing.
  • To send updates you have asked for. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (for example hosting, email and analytics) and only as needed, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above and take reasonable steps to protect it. No online system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing gwadar.digital@gmail.com.

Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
